# Solace: design and quality audit

**Date:** 2026-11-12
**Build:** release APK (74.5 MB), installed on `emulator-5554`
**Viewports:** 411 x 731 dp (1080 x 1920 px @ 420 dpi), 200% text scale, 731 x 411 dp wide
**Method:** Hallmark `audit` (Floor, Reflex, Finish; report only) and Impeccable native `audit` (5 dimensions, 0 to 4). Evidence: 17 screens, measured TalkBack bounds, 200% and wide captures, full source read.
**Not visually audited:** therapist list, detail and alert. `FLAG_SECURE` blackens captures by design; those screens were audited from source.
**Policy:** report only. No fixes were applied.
**Supersedes:** `apps/mobile/docs/audit.md`. That file predates the current build: it scores 13/20 and states that crisis numbers are never placeholder and that `FLAG_SECURE` is set globally; neither is true in the current tree.

---

## Verdict

**Hallmark: fails the Floor on six gates, plus one product rule that outranks every gate.**
**Impeccable: 11/20.**

The app has a serious motion and token foundation. What it does not yet have is its own voice: it renders in default Roboto with no Onest or Fraunces anywhere, the botanical stage is nearly invisible at 6% alpha, and its most safety-critical screen shows the literal string `UNVERIFIED` on a dead call button. That last one is the blocker.

A user in crisis meets placeholder data on the one screen the spec says must never contain it.

## What holds up

- **Token discipline.** Zero literal colours outside `tokens.g.dart` (only `Colors.transparent` for M3 tint suppression). No hard-coded durations in widgets.
- **Contrast.** Measured on the real tokens: heading 17.86:1, body 10.98:1, body on glass 9.26:1, leaf 13.09:1, accent 9.56:1, sage 6.88:1, inverse 13.33:1. All comfortably AA, most AAA.
- **Reduced motion.** `Reveal` jumps to its end frame, `BreathingOrb` stops and changes its prompt, and `BotanicalBackdrop` freezes (`motion.dart:81-88`, `breathing_orb.dart:52-58`, `motion.dart:202-209`). Transform and opacity only, as specified.
- **Semantics.** Every chip, tab, exit, and primary action in the inspected tree has a label and a role. The nav announces "Home, Tab 1 of 4".
- **Primary targets.** Buttons and icon targets are 44 x 44 dp (measured: Continue 384 x 48 dp, Exit 44 x 44 dp, nav item 103 x 64 dp).
- **Honesty.** No invented metrics, testimonials, or logo walls. Empty states say "No data yet" and mean it.
- **Therapist privacy.** `FLAG_SECURE` is correct on the therapist shell, and captures are black as intended.

## P0, fix before anyone sees this screen

### P0.1 The crisis screen presents unverified placeholders as the emergency path

`lib/features/crisis/crisis_screen.dart:151-163`, `assets/crisis/resources.json:11-21`

- Kaan Pete Roi renders a **disabled pill labelled `UNVERIFIED`**, with a call icon. The user's eye lands on a dead phone button. (999 still dials; the local helpline does not.)
- The 999 row renders `Unverified — 24 hours hours`: the template appends `l10n.crisisHours` to an `hours` field that already contains "hours", joined with an em dash.
- The Kaan Pete Roi row reads `Unverified — UNVERIFIED hours`.
- There is no visible way back. The only top-right control is the fast exit, which calls `SystemNavigator.pop()` and leaves the app (`crisis_screen.dart:129`).

`DESIGN.md:49` says "No silent success. Numbers over promises," and §10 puts crisis numbers in the offline floor. Placeholder rows violate both.

**Fix:** drop unverified rows at render time, or show them without a button and without the word UNVERIFIED in the action slot. Compose hours once. Add a visible "Back to your space" that navigates, not exits.

## P1, fix before the demo

### P1.1 Interactive chips are 29 dp tall

Measured from the accessibility tree at 420 dpi:

| Chip | Size (px) | Size (dp) |
|---|---|---|
| Low | 132 x 77 | 50.3 x 29.3 |
| Tender | 173 x 77 | 65.9 x 29.3 |
| Steady | 171 x 77 | 65.1 x 29.3 |
| Bright | 159 x 77 | 60.6 x 29.3 |
| Good | 149 x 77 | 56.8 x 29.3 |

Source: 6 dp vertical padding, no minimum size (`lib/design/widgets/chips_tabs.dart:22-29`). Used by check-in (`task_checkin_sheets.dart:200`), the crisis grounding shortcuts (`crisis_screen.dart:107-121`), and onboarding (`onboarding_screens.dart:202`).

This breaks the system's own rule (`DESIGN.md:35`, 44 x 44 dp) and Material's 48 dp minimum. On the check-in sheet the chips are the primary interaction.

**Fix:** wrap the visual pill in a `ConstrainedBox(minHeight: 44)` tap target, or `MaterialTapTargetSize.padded`. Keep the pill visual.

### P1.2 The type system is not implemented

`lib/app/theme.dart:12-95`, `pubspec.yaml:109-118`, `DESIGN.md:14-18`

- There are **no font files** in `assets/`, the `fonts:` block is commented out, and there is no `fontFamily`, `fontFamilyFallback`, or `textTheme` anywhere.
- Every screen falls back to Roboto. Fraunces never appears. Onest never appears.
- Because there is no `TextTheme`, unstyled body text inherits Material's 14 sp `bodyMedium`, while `DESIGN.md:18` requires body at 16 px or more.
- `theme.dart:7` admits it: "Type scale is Phase 3 work." That TODO now defines the product.

The design system's first paragraph calls the typography the voice; the app currently has no voice. This is the single largest reason the UI reads as generic dark Material rather than "Solace."

**Fix:** add Onest 400/500 and Fraunces 400/500, declare them, and build a `TextTheme` for display, headline, title, body, and label. Use Fraunces only for the accent/serif role.

### P1.3 Save and export claim success they do not deliver

- Journal: `_save()` shows "Saved on this device.", clears the field, and stores nothing (`journals_screens.dart:31-40`). Switching tabs disposes the controller, so even unsaved text is lost.
- Safety plan: Save shows "Safety plan saved on this device.", pops, and re-entering resets all five fields (`safety_plan_screen.dart:78-87`).
- Export: "Request export" shows "Export queued on this device." and queues nothing (`journals_screens.dart:324-325`).

`DESIGN.md:49`: "No silent success." A trauma journal that silently eats an entry is worse than one that says it cannot save yet.

**Fix:** persist locally, or say "Not saved in this demo" and stop clearing. Make export truthful.

### P1.4 `FLAG_SECURE` does not cover patient or crisis screens

`lib/main.dart:45`, `lib/app/router.dart:151-152`, `:228-233`, `DESIGN.md:53`

- The global `FlagSecure.setEnabled(true)` runs before `runApp`, so the method-channel call has no binding and is swallowed by the `catch` in `flag_secure.dart:10-12`. It is a no-op.
- Only the therapist `_SecureShell` enables the flag. When that shell disposes, it clears it again.
- Evidence: patient screenshots captured cleanly; therapist screenshots came out black. The flag is on for therapist and off everywhere else.

`DESIGN.md:53` requires it on every patient, therapist, and crisis screen. A journal screenshot on a shared device is a real privacy leak.

**Fix:** call `WidgetsFlutterBinding.ensureInitialized()` before the first secure call, or set `FLAG_SECURE` in `MainActivity`. Do not clear it on leaving the therapist shell.

### P1.5 The check-in sheet is not modal

`lib/features/home/home_screen.dart:42-50`

`showModalBottomSheet` is pushed on the nested navigator, so the bottom navigation stays live under the scrim. Verified: tapping Settings while the check-in sheet was open switched tabs and discarded the sheet. The user's input disappears with no warning.

**Fix:** `useRootNavigator: true`, or a full-screen route. The nav should sit under the barrier.

### P1.6 The "Did this help?" sheet encodes a preferred answer

`lib/features/home/task_checkin_sheets.dart:240-255`, `DESIGN.md:59`

"Yes, it helped" is a filled white primary; "Not really" is an outline. Two equal choices are visually unequal, and the labels differ in register (a direct yes, a softened no). In a trauma app, that is a nudge.

**Fix:** give both the same visual weight, and make the labels symmetrical.

### P1.7 Wide and landscape are stretched phone UI

`lib/app/patient_shell.dart:48-68`, `DESIGN.md:22`

At 731 x 411 dp:

- The Start pill is roughly **670 dp wide**, spanning the full card.
- The four nav destinations stretch across 731 dp with no rail.
- No content max-width appears; Settings in landscape is a full-width list.

`DESIGN.md:22` requires a capped, centred container. Material 3 expects a navigation rail at medium and expanded widths.

**Fix:** `LayoutBuilder` with a `NavigationRail` at 600 dp and a ~640 dp content clamp.

## P2, meaningful quality issues

1. **Bottom nav on detail screens, and it lies about location.** `/task` and `/safety-plan` live inside the shell, so the bar persists; `_indexFor` maps `/task` to Home and `/safety-plan` to Settings (`patient_shell.dart:22-31`). Screenshots a04 and a06 show Home and Settings lit on screens reached from Home. Move details out of the shell, or hide the bar and the highlight.
2. **Middle-dot meta strings.** `Therapist connection · Settings` (`home_screen.dart:262`), `Local demo account · no network` (`journals_screens.dart:192`), `Preview build · demo account already active` (`open_screen.dart:102`), `Placeholder · no clinical data` (`therapist_screens.dart:214`). Hallmark Floor 63, `DESIGN.md:42`.
3. **The crisis entry is labelled "Open".** `home_screen.dart:244-267`. It opens the most important screen in the app; say "Crisis numbers" or "Get help now". The footer duplicates the Settings tab and is the middle-dot string above.
4. **The safety plan is five unlabelled boxes.** `Field 1` to `Field 5`, five 2,000-character counters, 3-line boxes, and Save below the fold (`safety_plan_screen.dart:50-88`). "Field 4 is personal" is meaningless without named fields. Use prompts ("People I can call", "Places that feel safe"), hide counters until near the limit, pin Save.
5. **The grounding screen says its title twice.** App bar and body both render "Box breathing" (`grounding_screen.dart:49-62`, screenshot a03).
6. **Settings exposes the build.** Delete account sits fifth of eight (`journals_screens.dart:227-231`), "Offline shell" is developer vocabulary (`:237-240`), demo vocabulary appears four times, and Delete has no confirmation (`:330-342`). Group destructive actions last and confirm them; rename or hide the dev entries.
7. **The backdrop repaints the whole screen every frame.** On Home and `/open`, `BotanicalBackdrop` wraps the entire child inside the `CustomPaint` (`motion.dart:219-228`), then redraws 3 radial gradients and 9 newly allocated leaf paths at 60 fps on a 48 s loop (`:268-298`). The leaves are at 6% alpha; the cost buys almost nothing. Move the painter behind the content as a sibling layer and cache the static grid.
8. **The fast exit overlaps the companion.** `patient_shell.dart:41-45` pins it at top-right; the home header also puts the companion there. Visible at 200% (b01) and at 731 dp wide (c06), where the X sits over the sleep indicator. `DESIGN.md:35` promises 200% without collision.
9. **The disabled primary is 1.48:1.** `on_media` at 35% under `action_inverse_fg` at 55% (`buttons.dart:34-35`). The only action on the check-in sheet is practically invisible until a mood is picked (a02). Use a legible disabled tone, or keep the button hidden until enabled.
10. **The task screen has a dead band and skips the task.** A `Spacer` opens ~300 dp of void (`task_checkin_sheets.dart:70`), and Start immediately asks "Did this help?" without any task completion in Solace (`:28-39`). The first screen of the core loop should not ask for a verdict before the action.
11. **The home adds a 300 ms fake loader.** `home_screen.dart:29` shows the companion and "Preparing your space." on every mount, then switches. It is a flash for sighted users and an extra announcement for TalkBack. Remove it or tie it to real work.
12. **Therapist demo interactions are wrong, not just placeholder.** Checkbox taps ignore the tapped value and increment a counter (`therapist_screens.dart:313-330`): tapping the third checks the first, and nothing can be unchecked. "Mark reviewed" sets `Undoing...` forever (`:337-343`). Resolve is a no-op. Fine for a scaffold, but label it or fix the state machine.
13. **The therapist screens leak spec annotations into UI.** `Elapsed: (from server elapsedSeconds)`, `Trigger: (type only)`, `Recent mood: (from server)`, `Survey results: (full, therapist view)` (`therapist_screens.dart:299-306`, `:260-262`). Demo builds can say "demo"; they should not say "from server elapsedSeconds".

## P3, polish

- `AnimatedSize` in the unused `ExpandableTile` animates height (`chips_tabs.dart:177`), against `DESIGN.md:27`. Delete the widget or make it a fade.
- 11 sp meta labels (`home_screen.dart:112`, `open_screen.dart:104`). One step below the 12 sp caption floor; tiny at 320 dp.
- The offline screen stacks three pills, two of them primary (`open_screen.dart:45-73`), and puts a full sentence in a button label ("Here is a number you can call right now."). One primary, one sentence of supporting copy.
- `_InfoScreen` is a card at the top and a button at the bottom with 400+ dp of void between (`journals_screens.dart:441-451`). Privacy has no action at all, so it is a card and a void.
- "Therapist demo" runs flush into the nav with no bottom padding (`journals_screens.dart:243-258`, a10/a11).
- The 30-day mood empty state's "Check in" button navigates home instead of opening the check-in sheet (`journals_screens.dart:370-371`).
- GlassSurface says "used sparingly (nav bars, sheets)" but is the primary card container on home, crisis, task, and info screens (`surfaces.dart:5`). Either update the doc or reduce the usage; `DESIGN.md:21` actually says "no cards."

## Impeccable detail

**Accessibility (3/4).** The good: labels, roles, contrast, tap sizes on buttons/tabs/exit, reflow at 200% with no clipping. The gaps: 29 dp chips, a 200% collision, a 1.48:1 disabled CTA, and 11 sp meta text. Keyboard/D-pad focus not exercised, but Material defaults are intact.

**Performance (2/4).** The motion primitives are cheap and reduced-motion aware. The backdrop is not: on Home and `/open`, an infinite 60 fps repaint of a full-screen layer that includes the page content, plus per-frame `Path` allocation. Add the fake loader and the latent `AnimatedSize`, and the score is 2. No measured jank on the emulator; this is a battery and scroll-cost problem, not a visual one.

**Theming (2/4).** Colour is excellent. Type is absent. Radii drift between 14 (inputs), 16 (glass), 24 (sheet), and 999 (chips), with `StadiumBorder` doing the same job elsewhere. Two surface languages (glass cards and plain bands) without a rule. No text theme, no error styling, no dark/light pair. The design system exists in the tokens, not yet in the type.

**Conformance (2/4).** Material 3 components, haptics, back navigation, and snackbars are used correctly. But the nav bar appears on details with a wrong selection, the modal is not modal, destructive actions are unconfirmed and mid-list, and three actions claim success they do not deliver.

**Adaptivity (2/4).** 320 to 411 dp portrait works, 200% text reflows, safe areas are respected. Wide and landscape are not designed: no rail, no cap, a 670 dp primary button, and a 411 dp column stretched into 731 dp.

## Suggested order

1. **P0:** crisis data and layout. This is the demo blocker.
2. **P1:** chips to 44 dp; load the type system; make save/export truthful; fix `FLAG_SECURE`; make the sheet root-modal; equalize the help choices; add the wide layout.
3. **P2:** nav on details, crisis label and footer, safety plan fields, grounding duplicate, settings order and dev copy, backdrop repaint, 200% collision, disabled contrast.
4. **P3:** the rest.

If you want these applied, the matching Impeccable commands are:

- `$impeccable clarify` for the crisis, journal, and settings copy.
- `$impeccable typeset` for Onest/Fraunces and the text theme.
- `$impeccable harden` for persistence promises, export, `FLAG_SECURE`, and delete confirmation.
- `$impeccable adapt` for the rail and the max-width container.
- `$impeccable optimize` for the backdrop repaint and the loader.
- `$impeccable polish` for targets, radii, and spacing.

Hallmark itself only reports and redesigns; the fixes above are ordinary work in the project's own system.
